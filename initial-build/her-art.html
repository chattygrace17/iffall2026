Her Art

style and themes

Her style

The figures

Slim, androgynous people with large eyes and reddened knees and elbows, floating through other worlds.

The settings

Exotic animals in cities, with planets and stars in the sky.

A female point of view

She takes otaku culture, the world of manga and anime fans, and shows it from a woman's perspective.

A personal mythology

Takano describes her work as creating a personal mythology, a world of her own that keeps growing with each painting.
