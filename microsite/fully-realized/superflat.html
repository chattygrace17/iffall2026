Superflat

Murakami and Kaikai Kiki

What is Superflat?

A Japanese art movement started by Takashi Murakami. Superflat art uses flat, two-dimensional images taken from manga and anime, and it comments on consumer culture in Japan.

Murakami

After graduating, Takano became Murakami's assistant, and he was her first mentor. Murakami wanted to show the work of young artists and build a community around Superflat.

Kaikai Kiki

Murakami founded Kaikai Kiki in 2001 to support and manage young artists. Takano is one of its seven members, five of whom are women.

Key facts

  • Kaikai KikiFounded in 2001
  • SuperflatFlat art inspired by manga and anime
